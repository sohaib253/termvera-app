Private by design

Your client's contract never leaves your laptop.

Confidentiality clauses and IT policies stop most AI tools at the door. Termvera doesn't need the cloud, so it doesn't ask.

  • No upload. Documents, results and the database stay on your computer.
  • No third-party AI. Analysis runs on a built-in engine with published rules.
  • Works offline: on a rig, a client site or an air-gapped network.
  • No account, no password, nothing phoning home.

How it works

From a stamped PDF to a negotiation list, before the meeting.

01

Drop in the documents

The tender and your bid, or the contract and its amendments. Several files at once.

02

Termvera reads them

Text is extracted page by page, scans are OCR'd, and the contract is split into its numbered clauses.

03

Every clause is checked

Against the risk rules and the protections that should be there. Severity is calculated, not guessed.

04

You make the call

Review each finding at its source, accept or override, and export the risk register to Excel.

Published checklists

Know exactly what it checks.

Every rule says why a clause matters and what to ask for instead. The full lists are in the app, under Help.

Contract risk

LIA-01Uncapped liabilityCritical

Negotiate a cap, commonly a multiple of contract price or the insured limit.

COM-02Pay-when-paidCritical

Delete it, or cap the waiting period and keep the right to be paid regardless of upstream insolvency.

LIA-05Broad indemnityHigh

Limit it to losses caused by your negligence or breach, in proportion to fault and within the cap.
